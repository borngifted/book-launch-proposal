## Registrations & Accounts Roadmap

Everything required to publish, in order. Work Official handles setup and filing concierge-style; government/registry fees are pass-through at cost.

### Required to publish

| # | Registration | Where | Cost (2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Amazon KDP account** | kdp.amazon.com | Free | Includes tax interview (W-9) + bank details for royalties |
| 2 | **ISBN** (one per format: eBook, paperback, hardcover) | Bowker (myidentifiers.com) | $125 single / $295 for 10 | Own ISBNs recommended — required for wide distribution under your imprint. (KDP's free ISBN works only on Amazon and lists Amazon as publisher.) |
| 3 | **U.S. Copyright registration** | copyright.gov | $45 single application / $65 standard | Fee increase proposed for fall 2026 — file early |
| 4 | **LCCN / PCN** (Library of Congress Control Number) | loc.gov PrePub Book Link | Free | Must apply **before** publication; mail one copy after release |
| 5 | **Barcode** (print editions) | Bowker or generated with cover | $0–$25 | KDP generates one free on the cover if price-encoding isn't needed |

### Recommended for a real publishing business

| # | Registration | Where | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Publishing imprint** (LLC or DBA) | State filing | ~$50–$150 (state fee) | Your books publish under your imprint, not your personal name |
| 7 | **EIN** | IRS | Free | For the imprint's tax interview and banking |
| 8 | **Amazon Author Central** | author.amazon.com | Free | Author page, bio, photo — links all your titles |
| 9 | **Goodreads Author Program** | goodreads.com | Free | Claim the book, run giveaways |
| 10 | **BookBub Author profile** | bookbub.com | Free | Deal alerts + follower base |

### Wide distribution (beyond Amazon)

## Scope of Work

### Phase 1 — Registrations & Publishing Setup (Weeks 1–2)
- File all required registrations (ISBN block, copyright, LCCN, barcode)
- Form publishing imprint + EIN (if selected)
- Create and configure KDP account: tax interview, royalty banking, publisher profile
- Set up Author Central, Goodreads, BookBub profiles

### Phase 2 — Book Publication (Weeks 2–4)
- Interior formatting coordination (eBook + print-ready PDF)
- Cover spec validation for KDP + Ingram (spine width, bleed)
- Metadata optimization: title/subtitle, 7 keywords, category selection, book description (HTML-formatted)
- Pricing strategy across formats and marketplaces
- Upload, preview, proof, and publish on KDP; wide distribution setup if selected

### Phase 3 — Author Platform Website (Weeks 3–6)
- Custom showcase site: hero, book preview/sample chapter, author bio, press kit, blog-ready
- **Buy-Now hub**: every retailer the book is live on, one universal link
- Email capture forms wired to CRM (reader magnet / sample-chapter opt-in)
- Social feeds and share links integrated
- SEO fundamentals: meta tags, Open Graph, schema.org Book markup
- Hosted on GitHub Pages (fast, free hosting) with custom domain

### Phase 4 — Social & CRM Engine (Weeks 5–6)
- Social profiles connected and standardized (bios, links, branding)
- Scheduling/analytics tool configured (Metricool)
- CRM configured: reader list, tags, signup automation
- 3 core email automations: welcome sequence, launch announcement, review request

### Not included (available as add-ons)
- Manuscript editing/proofreading, cover design from scratch, paid advertising spend, audiobook narration, ongoing monthly management (see Add-ons).
